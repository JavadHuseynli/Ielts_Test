The world has many amazing natural features. Here are some of the most beautiful
and famous ones.
Uluru, which is also called Ayers Rock, is in Australia. It is a huge rock in the
middle of a desert. Its usual colour is browny-red, but sometimes it looks bright
red. The highest point is 863 metres above sea level. In the past, lots of tourists
climbed Uluru, but usually not in the summer because it can be 47 degrees Celsius
or more. However, soon it won’t be possible to climb Uluru. Local people in
Australia feel Uluru is special, so they want to protect it.
Lake Baikal in Russia is another wonderful natural feature. Scientists say it’s the
world’s deepest lake. It’s also the world’s oldest freshwater lake. It may be 25
million years old. More than 330 rivers and streams bring water into Lake Baikal.
There’s so much water, in fact, that around 20 percent of all the fresh water on
Earth is in Lake Baikal. All that water makes the area near Lake Baikal warmer. In
summer, the average temperature is 11–14 degrees Celsius. This is cold, but it’s
warmer than areas that are far from the lake.
Niagara Falls is partly in Canada and partly in the USA. It’s probably the most
famous waterfall in the world. However, it’s not the biggest. Victoria Falls between
Zambia and Zimbabwe is wider and more powerful than other waterfalls. It’s taller
than Niagara, but it isn’t the highest waterfall. Angel Falls in Venezuela is much
higher. At Victoria Falls, the water falls 100 metres, but at Angel Falls, the water
falls almost 1,000 metres.
The Colorado River is a long river in the USA and Mexico. In one area, the river
cuts through the rock to make a very deep valley called the Grand Canyon. The
rock is hard, so this took a very long time. These days, the Grand Canyon is very
popular with tourists from all over the world. Some people take a boat down the
river. Other people walk from the top to the bottom. But most people just want to
look at the beautiful views.
