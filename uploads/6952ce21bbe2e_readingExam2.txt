Weekend Plans 
Sofia is a university student. She doesn’t have classes on Saturdays and Sundays, so weekends are her favorite days. On Saturday mornings, Sofia likes to sleep late. After breakfast, she sometimes goes shopping with her best friend, Mia. They love trying on clothes and buying snacks at the mall.
In the afternoon, Sofia often visits her grandparents. They live in a small house with a big garden. Sofia’s grandmother makes delicious cakes, and Sofia helps her in the kitchen.
On Sundays, Sofia likes to relax at home. She reads books, watches movies, or listens to music. In the evening, she always prepares for the week ahead. She studies for her classes and organizes her things. Sofia thinks weekends are a great time to rest and spend time with family and friends.
