FORMS OF COMMUNICATION: 
EMAIL, INSTANT MESSAGES, FACE-TO-FACE, AND PHONE
Communication is very important in our daily lives. We use different ways to share information, ideas, and feelings. Each form of communication has its own advantages and disadvantages. Here are four common types of communication: email, instant messages, face-to-face, and phone.
Email is a popular way to send messages. It is fast, and you can send it to many people at the same time. You can also attach files, like pictures or documents. However, emails can sometimes feel formal, and people may take a long time to reply.
Instant messages (IM) are great for quick conversations. Apps like WhatsApp and Messenger allow people to chat in real time. IM is less formal than email, and it is easy to use. However, it can be distracting because people may expect quick replies.
Talking face-to-face is the best way to understand someone. You can see their expressions and hear their voice. It feels personal and helps build strong relationships. But face-to-face communication is not always possible, especially if the person is far away.
Phone calls are a good way to talk when you cannot meet in person. You can hear the person’s voice, which makes the conversation clear. However, phone calls can sometimes be expensive, and you cannot see the other person’s facial expressions.
Each form of communication is useful in different situations. It is important to choose the best method depending on what you need.
