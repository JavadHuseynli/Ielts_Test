Mark`s New Bike
Mark is 12 years old, and he loves riding bikes. Last week, his parents bought him a new red bike for his birthday. Mark was very happy and wanted to try it right away. 
On Saturday morning, Mark woke up early and went to the park with his bike. The park is near his house, and it has a lot of space for cycling. Mark rode his bike for two hours. He also met his friends, Sam and Lucy, who were playing football. They asked Mark to join them, but he said, "Maybe later. I want to ride my bike more!"
In the afternoon, Mark went home and cleaned his bike. His father helped him because he wanted the bike to look new for a long time. After dinner, Mark told his family about his day. He said, "I love my new bike! Thank you so much!"
