THE HISTORY OF TESLA – SIMPLIFIED VERSION
Tesla Motors was founded in 2003 by engineers Martin Eberhard and Marc Tarpenning. They started the company to build electric sports cars. In 2004, Elon Musk joined Tesla as a major investor and became the chairman. His support helped the company grow.
Tesla released its first car, the Roadster, in 2008. It was the first highway-legal electric car with lithium-ion batteries and could travel over 200 miles on a single charge. In 2012, Tesla launched the Model S, a luxury electric car known for its speed, safety, and long battery range. The Model X SUV followed in 2015 and became famous for its unique “Falcon Wing” doors.
In 2017, Tesla introduced the Model 3, a more affordable car. It became one of the best-selling electric cars worldwide. In 2020, Tesla added the Model Y, a compact SUV. To meet growing demand, Tesla built large factories called Gigafactories in Nevada, Shanghai, and Berlin to produce batteries and vehicles more efficiently.
Tesla has also been a leader in car technology. It released Autopilot, an advanced driver-assistance system, in 2014, and has been developing Full Self-Driving features. In 2010, Tesla became a public company by joining the NASDAQ stock exchange. By the early 2020s, it was one of the world’s most valuable carmakers.
Today, Tesla continues to work on new products like solar roof tiles, energy storage systems, and the Cybertruck. Its mission is to help the world switch to sustainable energy.
