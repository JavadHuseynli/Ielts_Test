A Day Trip

Last weekend, my friend Jane and I decided to visit a small town. The town was by the sea, and it had beautiful beaches. We left early on Saturday morning and after about an hour of traveling, we arrived in the town. First, we went to the beach and took a walk along the shore. The weather was perfect, sunny and warm with a light breeze. It was a lovely day.

We had lunch at a seaside restaurant. I ordered pizza, and Jane ordered fish. We both really enjoyed the food. After lunch, we walked slowly to the town center. There were a few small shops. Jane  bought a nice dress, and I bought a piece of jewelry.

In the afternoon, we went back to the beach to watch the sunset. The view was amazing. We ended the day feeling happy and relaxed. Our trip that day was really great, and we can’t wait to go again.
